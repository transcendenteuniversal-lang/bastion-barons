## Description
Defend your castle and destroy your enemy's in fast real-time battles across three lanes!

Bastion Barons mixes card collecting with lane strategy. Pick a Baron, build your deck and send knights, elven archers, necromancers, dragons and powerful spells onto the battlefield. Your troops march and fight on their own, but where and when you play each card decides the battle.

Features:
- 3 lanes, fast decisions: read the battlefield and answer every attack in the right lane.
- 5 legendary Barons, each with a unique power: Royal Command, Elven Rain, Orc Fury, Army of the Dead and Arcane Storm.
- 5 castles with their own powers, like Imperial Shield, Ice Storm, Dragon Breath and Soul Drain.
- 42 cards to collect and upgrade, from cheap skeletons to legendary dragons and the mythic Phoenix.
- Progress that rewards you: earn gold, XP and new cards with every win, and level up to unlock new Barons and castles.
- Daily rewards that grow every day you come back, plus double rewards for your first win of the day.
- A quick tutorial teaches you everything in your first battle.
- Three AI difficulties: Easy, Normal and Hard.

Your progress is saved automatically.

## Controls
Mouse / touch:
- Drag a card from your hand onto a lane to summon troops or cast a spell.
- Drag your Baron's power (round button, bottom left) onto a lane when it is charged.
- Drag your castle's power onto a lane when it is ready.
- Drag a card onto the bin to discard it and draw the next one.
- Pause button (top left) or Esc: pause / go back.

## Which tools did you use?
