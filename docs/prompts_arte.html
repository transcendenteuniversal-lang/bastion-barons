Prompts de personagens — Bastion Barons

  • Anexe a imagem da carta (a que aparece em cada bloco) como referência junto com o prompt.
  • Uma imagem por carta: só o personagem (ou o efeito da magia), com todos os detalhes. A animação vem depois.
  • Fundo verde; personagens verdes (goblin, orc, ent, druida, elfa) e o veneno vão em magenta para o recorte não apagar o personagem.
  • Mande cada folha como anexo de arquivo e diga de qual carta é.
1. Cavaleiro Real
Tropa
Character design reference sheet for a 2D mobile battle game: ONE single full-body character, a sturdy royal knight in blue and gold plate armor with a fleur-de-lis shield and a short sword. Stylized 3D cartoon, Castle Crush / Castle Clash style: clean shapes, few colors, bold readable silhouette, crisp edges, high definition. FAITHFUL COPY OF THE ATTACHED CARD ART: this is the same character as in the attached card image, do NOT redesign it. Keep exactly the same face and expression, skin color, hair or helmet, body proportions, armor shapes, outfit, colors and color placement, patterns and emblems, weapon shape and size, and accessories. Do not add or remove any item, do not change the age, style or color palette, do not make it cuter or more realistic; only show the full body that the card does not show, continuing the same design consistently. Show only this one character, standing in a neutral ready pose, full body from head to feet, seen from the side (3/4 side view) facing RIGHT, holding its weapon (if it has one) in its normal position. Show every detail clearly: face, hair, armor, clothes, colors, patterns, weapon and accessories (whatever it has), so this exact design can be reused later for the animations. Perfectly flat solid chroma GREEN background (#00FF00), no gradients, no vignette, no shadow on the background, no floor, no text, no numbers, no labels, no border. Wide landscape image 16:9. The character stands in the exact center of the image and takes only about 45% of the image height, with very wide empty background space on all four sides (much more on the left and right) so later the walking and attack animations, swinging weapons, open wings, tails, capes and effects will never be cut or touch the edge. Sharp, clean, high definition, even lighting.
